NeuroX AI

Privacy, explained clearly.

Last updated: 12 November 2026

This page explains how the NeuroX AI web app handles account access, chat history and AI requests. It is a plain-language product notice, not legal advice.

Account sign-in

When you sign in or create an account, authentication is handled by Firebase Authentication. NeuroX AI uses the signed-in account state to separate locally saved conversations in your browser.

Where chat history is stored

Conversation history is saved in your browser's local storage on this device. Signed-in users have a separate local history key for their account. History is not currently synchronised between devices or stored in a NeuroX AI cloud database. Clearing this browser's site data can delete saved conversations.

How AI requests work

When you send a prompt, the browser sends the prompt and a limited amount of recent conversation context to the NeuroX AI backend. The backend routes the request to whichever configured AI provider is selected or available (for example, Groq, OpenRouter or Google Gemini) and returns the answer. The relevant provider may process the prompt under its own privacy and retention terms.

API keys and security

AI provider secret keys are configured on the backend hosting service and are not included in the frontend code. Do not enter passwords, payment details, private keys or other highly sensitive information into chat prompts. The chat API also applies request-size limits and rate limiting, but no internet service can promise absolute security.

Attachments

The current attachment control previews selected images in the browser. Image content is not currently sent to the AI model as part of the chat request.

Your choices

  • Use the Clear chat control to clear the active conversation.
  • Use Export to save a Markdown copy of the active conversation.
  • Clear site data in your browser to remove all locally stored NeuroX AI conversations and preferences.
  • Sign out from your account menu when using a shared device.

For product or privacy questions, contact the developer through GitHub.
